About the academy

Coached by someone who also runs the tournaments.

Promod's Chess Academy is led by Promodraj Moree, who coaches players locally and also handles ratings and pairings for the Gulbarga District Chess Association — meaning the training here is shaped by first-hand knowledge of what district and state arbiters actually look for.

Programs

A path from first moves to first rating.

Each program is built around a stage of a player's development, from learning how the pieces move to preparing for a FIDE or AICF-rated event.

e2–e4

Foundations

Rules, tactics, and board vision for complete beginners, taught in small groups with plenty of supervised play.

Nf3–e5

Improvers

Opening principles, endgame technique, and calculation drills for players ready to compete in local tournaments.

O-O

Tournament prep

Repertoire building, time management, and mock rated games for students entering AICF and KSCA-rated events.
